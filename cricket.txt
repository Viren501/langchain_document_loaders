**The Eternal Game of Cricket**

In the golden haze of morning light,  
Where the sun paints the pitch in silver white,  
A leather sphere, red as summer’s blood,  
Waits for the bowler’s determined thud.  
The stumps stand tall like silent guards,  
Three wooden soldiers, watching hard.  
The crease is drawn, a sacred line,  
Where hope and fear forever intertwine.  

The batsman walks with measured pace,  
A warrior’s calm upon his face.  
Helmet gleaming, gloves pulled tight,  
He takes his guard against the light.  
The fielders scatter, eyes like hawks,  
Each one a hunter in the box.  
Silly point crouches low and keen,  
While mid-off dreams of catches clean.  

The bowler turns, a long run starts,  
A storm of limbs and beating hearts.  
Arm swings high, a cobra’s strike,  
The ball takes flight in pure delight.  
It swings, it seams, it whispers lies,  
It dances under open skies.  
The bat comes down with thunder’s roar,  
A crack that echoes evermore.  

Four runs race to the boundary rope,  
The crowd erupts in songs of hope.  
Or maybe six, a soaring bird,  
That vanishes without a word.  
The scoreboard ticks, the numbers climb,  
Like verses in an endless rhyme.  
Each over ends, a chapter closed,  
Yet still the story is composed.  

From dusty maidans of the East,  
To Lord’s, the game’s eternal feast.  
From Bradman’s blade so pure and true,  
To Tendulkar’s every hundred new.  
From Warne’s spin that twisted fate,  
To Ambrose’s pace that sealed the gate.  
From Kapil’s crown in ’83,  
To Kohli’s fire for you and me.  

The children play with plastic bats,  
In narrow lanes and concrete flats.  
A broken chair becomes the stump,  
A tennis ball the only jump.  
They dream of stadiums vast and grand,  
Of holding trophies in their hand.  
They learn the language of the game—  
The leave, the drive, the soft disclaimer.  

Rain may fall and stop the play,  
Yet still the spirit finds a way.  
Under covers, green and tight,  
The pitch sleeps through the rainy night.  
When sun returns, the game resumes,  
With wet grass and forgotten blooms.  
The umpires stand with quiet grace,  
The keepers of this sacred space.  

A single run can change the tide,  
A dropped catch leaves no place to hide.  
A century built brick by brick,  
A hat-trick’s sudden, brutal trick.  
The nightwatchman holds the fort,  
While legends write their last report.  
The tail-enders swing with glee,  
As if the world were theirs to free.  

In Test matches, time stands still,  
Five days of will against the will.  
In T20’s blaze of neon light,  
The game becomes a pure delight.  
In ODIs the balance holds,  
Between the new and stories old.  
Each format sings a different song,  
Yet all belong where they belong.  

The crowd becomes a living sea,  
Of flags and chants and pure esprit.  
The Mexican wave that rolls and dies,  
The silence when a wicket flies.  
The gasp that follows every edge,  
The roar that greets the winning pledge.  
Mothers, fathers, sons and friends,  
All bound by cricket’s timeless trends.  

When stumps are drawn and lights go low,  
The players leave, but memories grow.  
The empty stands still hold the sound  
Of leather on willow, profound.  
The scorebooks close, the ink grows dry,  
Yet every match refuses to die.  
It lives in stories told at night,  
In dreams that keep the pure delight.  

So raise a glass to bat and ball,  
To every rise and every fall.  
To captains calm and bowlers wild,  
To every woman, man and child  
Who ever felt the game’s embrace,  
Who ever ran between the space  
Of crease and crease, of hope and fear,  
Of summer’s joy and winter’s tear.  

Cricket is more than just a sport—  
It is a language, a report  
Of courage, patience, skill and grace,  
Of time that leaves no empty space.  
It teaches loss and how to rise,  
It shows the truth behind the eyes.  
It binds the world in one long song,  
That plays forever, loud and strong.  

And when the final ball is bowled,  
And every story has been told,  
The game will still be waiting there—  
In every heart, in every air.  
For cricket never truly ends;  
It only pauses, then transcends.  
A red ball flying through the sky,  
A game that teaches us to try.
